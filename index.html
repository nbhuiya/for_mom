To Tell You Something ❤️

Mamma ❤️

Thank you for never letting me give up, even during the toughest phases of life.

Thank you for not blindly supporting every decision I made, but instead correcting me whenever I was wrong, even if it meant scolding me.

Thank you for protecting me from the world before I even understood how cruel it could be.

You knew me 9 months before the world did, and even today nobody understands me the way you do.

Thank you for never forcing me into household responsibilities when I had exams, and instead scolding me whenever I entered the kitchen to waste time.

But still, whenever I wanted to cook out of love or happiness, you always allowed me to do it.

You didn’t just allow me to dream, you pushed me towards becoming someone special, someone beyond ordinary.

I know you’ll probably scold me for making all this, but how could I miss a chance to make something for the person who made my whole world magical!! ❤️

Whatever strength I have today, a huge part of it is because of you.

With Love,

From Shona Mamma ❤️
